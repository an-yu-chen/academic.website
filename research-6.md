<h2>📚 A new layer to my research</h2>

<p>
  In August, 2026, I successfully completed the
  <strong>WIPO Academy General Course on Intellectual Property (DL-101)</strong>.
  The course provided a structured opportunity to strengthen my
  understanding of intellectual property and its relationship with
  innovation.
</p>

<p>
  For my research, this was particularly useful because intellectual
  property connects closely with questions I already study: how knowledge
  is created, shared, protected, and transformed into innovation.
</p>

</div> <!-- MAIN TAKEAWAY --> <div class="ip-highlight">
<strong>My main takeaway:</strong><br>

Innovation is not only about producing new knowledge. It is also about
the institutions and rules that influence how that knowledge can move,
be protected, and be used.

</div> <!-- RESEARCH CONNECTIONS --> <div class="ip-section">
<h2>🔎 Connecting with my research</h2>

<p>
  The course adds an intellectual property perspective to several areas
  of my research.
</p>


<div class="research-point">

  <strong>Science–technology linkages</strong>

  <p>
    My work examines how scientific knowledge becomes connected to
    technological development. Intellectual property adds an important
    institutional dimension to this relationship, helping me think about
    how knowledge can move from research into technological applications.
  </p>

</div>


<div class="research-point">

  <strong>Knowledge infrastructure and policy</strong>

  <p>
    My research considers how institutions and information systems shape
    knowledge production and circulation. Intellectual property systems
    form part of this wider infrastructure and provide another lens for
    understanding how knowledge is organised and used.
  </p>

</div>


<div class="research-point">

  <strong>Gender and knowledge flow</strong>

  <p>
    My work also considers who receives visibility, recognition, access,
    and opportunities within systems of knowledge production and
    innovation. Understanding the institutional rules surrounding
    knowledge is therefore important when examining inequalities within
    innovation systems.
  </p>

</div>

</div> <!-- RESEARCH DEVELOPMENT --> <div class="ip-section">
<h2>🌱 From learning to research</h2>

<p>
  I see this course as part of my continuing development as a researcher.
  Rather than treating intellectual property as a separate topic, I want
  to connect it with my broader interest in knowledge, technology, and
  innovation policy.
</p>

<p>
  This perspective will be useful as I continue working across
  bibliometrics, science of science, innovation studies, and policy
  research.
</p>
